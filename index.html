About me

I am an undergraduate student in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Peking University, expecting to graduate in July 2029.

My long-term research goal is to understand the principles underlying deep learning and neural networks, and to use this understanding to design better algorithms and models. My current interests include foundations of deep learning, LLM architectures, generative models, and reinforcement learning.

I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Ziming Liu at Tsinghua University.

Please feel free to get in touch!
